Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026 · See also the Terms of Service

woometer is a small hobby site. It keeps as little about you as it can, never sells anything, and shows no ads. Here's everything it stores and why.

What's stored

  • Your answers. The Yes, No or Don't Know you gave each claim, and when.
  • An anonymous id. When you first visit, you quietly get a random account id so your answers can be saved and counted. It isn't tied to your name or email unless you sign in.
  • Your Google name, email and picture, only if you sign in with Google. Used to show who's signed in and to keep your answers and friends on every device. woometer never sees your Google password and never posts anything for you.
  • A display name, if you type one, so friends know who you are.
  • Your friend link and friend list. A random code for your share link, and the people you've added as friends.
  • Your rough location and a scrambled code of your IP address. Each time you visit, woometer notes the approximate country, region and city your internet connection comes from (a guess, not your exact spot) and when you visited. Your IP address itself is never kept: it's turned into a scrambled code that can't be turned back into the address. The location shows where in the world people are answering from, and the code lets woometer spot a flood of fake answers from one place and keep them out of the stats. Only the site owner can see these, in the database. They're never shown on the site, in totals or in shared links.
  • In your own browser, a copy of your answers, your sound setting and the sign-in session, so the page works offline and remembers you.

Who can see it

  • Everyone, only as totals. The "N% of people agree" numbers and the Stats page count all answers together and never show who gave them.
  • Anyone you give your link to can see your display name, woo score and answers. Only share it with people you're happy to show.
  • Your friends can see your display name and answers, to compare with theirs.
  • Your email address and location are never shown to other visitors.

Who handles it

  • Supabase stores the accounts and answers for woometer. It only processes them to run the site.
  • Google handles "Sign in with Google" if you use it.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile may run a quick, usually invisible check that you're a person, to keep bots from skewing the numbers.
  • The pages themselves are served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses.

woometer uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Deleting your data

  • Signed in with Google? Open the account menu (your picture at the top of the page) and choose Delete my account. That permanently deletes your account, answers, name, friend link, friend list and visit records.
  • Never signed in? Start over, under your Trash Bin, Believe It and Don't Know lists on the main page, deletes all your answers from woometer's server and from this browser.

Children

woometer isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
